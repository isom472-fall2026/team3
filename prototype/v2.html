KU Bus Tracker, prototype v2

Every screen a student or coordinator uses in Phases 4–6, drawn with the colours already in index.css. Tap anything in the phone: the map buses, the routes, the forms and the cancel button all respond.

Screens

    Five rules for every page

    • Gold means a bus. KU gold is used for nothing else, so a student spots a bus at a glance.
    • Each route keeps its colour. South is KU blue, North is KU black, on the map, the badges and the line diagram.
    • Times are the biggest thing on the screen. Minutes away beats the clock time, because that is the question students ask.
    • Lists, not stacks of cards. Rows with dividers inside one white panel. A card is used only when it is the one thing on screen.
    • Every list has three more states. Loading, empty and error each say what is happening and what to do next. A database error never reaches the screen.

    Colour

    --ku-blue, South route and selection
    --ku-black, North route and buttons

    Type is Overpass, a face drawn from road-sign lettering, with tabular numbers so times line up. If the team prefers no web font, the app's system font works with the same sizes.
